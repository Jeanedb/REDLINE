Operational Security Review

Identify process vulnerabilities, enhance awareness, and reduce operational exposure through focused security reviews.

What We Offer

Our Operational Security Review (OPSEC) service evaluates the effectiveness of your current procedures, staff behavior, and internal safeguards. We identify areas where sensitive information could be inadvertently exposed or exploited, and provide mitigation strategies that align with your organizational mission and threat profile.

Assessment Focus Areas:

  • Security culture and procedural awareness
  • Internal communication and data handling
  • Behavioral patterns and routine exposure
  • Access and privilege management
  • Contractor and third-party access review

Benefits to Your Organization

  • Reduce risk of internal data leakage
  • Harden operational procedures
  • Align practices with your mission security posture
  • Improve employee vigilance and buy-in
  • Close gaps before adversaries exploit them

Ideal Clients

This service is especially valuable for:

  • Organizations with sensitive workflows
  • Government contractors and classified operations
  • Facilities with frequent third-party access
  • Companies managing proprietary technologies
  • Healthcare and data-driven businesses

Sample Finding

Issue: Sensitive floor plans were being stored in an unsecured shared drive.
Impact: High risk of internal exposure or external breach.
Recommendation: Restrict access permissions and transfer sensitive files to secure document management systems.

Deliverables

  • OPSEC profile overview and threat summary
  • Identification of behavioral and procedural gaps
  • Annotated findings with photo references (if applicable)
  • Mitigation recommendations and SOP enhancements
  • Training alignment and awareness briefing templates
