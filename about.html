About Redline Consulting

We deliver elite physical security and consulting services tailored to your mission. Since 2008, Redline Consulting has stood for integrity, excellence, and innovation.

Our Story

Redline Consulting was born from the boots-on-the-ground experience of military veterans, seasoned law enforcement officers, and risk management professionals who’ve spent their careers protecting lives and assets in dynamic, high-risk environments.

From safeguarding critical infrastructure to overseeing corporate security across sprawling campuses, we’ve operated quietly but effectively—ensuring safety, continuity, and confidence behind the scenes. Our approach is rooted in reality: we know that true protection isn’t passive. It’s not just about cameras, fences, or access control—it’s about foresight, adaptability, and training people to respond when it matters most.

At our core, we believe security is not a product—it’s a mindset. And that mindset, forged in real-world operations, is what sets Redline apart.
