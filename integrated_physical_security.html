Integrated Physical Security

Build a fortress mindset. Redline delivers comprehensive physical protection through layered security technologies and strategic placement of systems that prevent, detect, and respond to threats.

What We Offer

Our Integrated Physical Security service blends advanced surveillance, robust access controls, and physical barriers to create a seamless defense strategy. We assess, design, and implement protection that matches your operational demands and site vulnerabilities.

Core Components

  • CCTV and 24/7 surveillance systems
  • Controlled access with audit trails
  • Barrier systems (bollards, gates, turnstiles)
  • Intrusion detection and response sensors
  • Integration with security personnel protocols

Why It Matters

  • Deter unauthorized access before it begins
  • Gain real-time situational control
  • Streamline investigations with recorded data
  • Reduce liability with traceable systems
  • Boost insurance and regulatory compliance

Ideal Clients

We specialize in delivering these solutions to:

  • Corporate campuses and headquarters
  • Healthcare and data centers
  • Warehouses and logistics hubs
  • Retail chains and public venues
  • Government and critical infrastructure

Example Solution

Problem: An urban distribution facility experienced repeated night-time perimeter breaches.
Solution: Installed motion-triggered thermal cameras, electronic locks, and vehicle-rated bollards.
Result: Zero breaches reported in 9 months, with improved shift accountability and reduced theft.
