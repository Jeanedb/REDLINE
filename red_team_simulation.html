Red Team Simulations

Test your defenses with adversarial simulations that reveal real vulnerabilities in your security posture—before an actual threat does.

What We Do

Redline's Red Team operations simulate realistic threat actor behavior to test your organization’s detection and response capabilities. From covert entry to insider threat tactics, we uncover weaknesses across physical, procedural, and personnel domains.

Scenarios We Conduct

  • Covert access attempts and bypass testing
  • Impersonation and social engineering
  • Insider threat identification
  • Response force timing & effectiveness drills
  • After-hours intrusion simulations

Why It Matters

  • Uncover real-world blind spots
  • Train staff under live pressure conditions
  • Strengthen policy compliance and awareness
  • Evaluate contractor and third-party readiness
  • Enhance layered defense strategies

Who's It For

Organizations that benefit most from Red Teaming:

  • Critical infrastructure & utilities
  • Financial institutions
  • Corporate campuses
  • Defense and aerospace facilities
  • High-profile event venues

Example Engagement

Scenario: Simulated social engineering attack targeting front desk staff.
Result: One entry granted without ID verification.
Impact: Immediate retraining and SOP revision. Future tests showed marked improvement in protocol enforcement.

What You'll Receive

  • Complete engagement brief and objectives
  • Detailed timeline of simulated breaches
  • Photos and video (if applicable)
  • Scoring matrix and risk rating
  • Corrective action recommendations

Case Study Highlight

One client discovered a previously unknown fire exit vulnerability exploited in a late-night simulation. Remediation led to updated locks, improved signage, and annual night-time drills.
